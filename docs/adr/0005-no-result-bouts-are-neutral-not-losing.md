# A No Result Bout is neutral inside a Chained Entry, not a loss

When a Bout is cancelled, a fighter withdraws, or the fight ends in a draw or no contest,
the Prediction on that Bout contributes a Multiplier of ×1.0 and the rest of the Chained
Entry plays on. If every Prediction in an Entry is No Result, the Amount is refunded in
full.

Treating it as a loss would punish users for something no prediction could have anticipated
— fight cards change constantly, and a withdrawn fighter would silently kill every Chained
Entry containing them.

Disqualification is handled differently and deliberately: the **winner** Question settles
normally, because the DQ winner did win, while the method and round Questions become No
Result, since "won by DQ" is not one of the three methods offered.
